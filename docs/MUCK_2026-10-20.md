## General architecture decision

Correctness precedes single-game success. Classify blockers as Mavk, Sarek,
ABI/WSI or external runtime. These two changes are in shared Mavk APIs, not Muck
rules: instrumentation cannot silently require unavailable hardware writes;
resource metadata must normalize supported pNext semantics, not discard them.
The registry and emitted native snapshots must share the same admission rules
and retain native format/aspect/usage semantics. Fixing only one gate gives a
false local PASS and moves the same defect downstream; review the whole path.
Diagnostic reporting is optional and separate from access protection. Native
feature bits remain unchanged. Unknown shapes still reject rather than claiming
robustness2/nullDescriptor. Next expand only operations blocked by real consumers,
with the corresponding bounded regression and real consumer rerun. A D3D11
device or background image is not a universal/complete Vulkan or playable-game
claim. Broader typed views/null formats and explicit early admission remain
unimplemented beyond the documented supported subset.
